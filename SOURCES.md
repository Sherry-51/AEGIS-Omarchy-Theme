# AEGIS — Wallpaper Sources & Licensing Documentation

This document records the provenance, authorship, licensing, and attribution details for all **16 curated wallpapers** included in the **AEGIS — Deep Space Command Center** theme for Omarchy Linux.

---

## Attribution & Provenance Summary

The collection consists of:
1. **Public Domain Scientific Imagery (11 Wallpapers):** Authentic space photography and scientific instrument visualizations sourced directly from the **NASA Image and Video Library** (`images.nasa.gov`), **NASA Goddard Space Flight Center**, **NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory (JPL-Caltech)**, **Space Telescope Science Institute (STScI)**, and the **European Space Agency (ESA)**. As works of the United States Government created by NASA personnel, these images are in the **Public Domain** under Title 17, U.S. Code § 105.
2. **Original AEGIS Bespoke Vector & Telemetry Imagery (5 Wallpapers):** High-precision vector, orbital trajectory, and deep-space minimalist backgrounds designed specifically for the AEGIS workstation theme, licensed under the **MIT License**.

All images have been formatted, color-graded, and calibrated to **2560×1440 (16:9)** resolution to harmonize with the AEGIS palette (`#080B14` base, deep space blue, ice cyan highlights).
